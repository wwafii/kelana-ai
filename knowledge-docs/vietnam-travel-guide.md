# Vietnam Travel Guide, e-Visa Regulations & Local Etiquette (2025/2026)

## 1. Visa Policies & 90-Day Electronic Visa
- **ASEAN Exemption**: Indonesian passport holders enjoy a 30-day visa exemption for tourism.
- **90-Day e-Visa**: Citizens of all countries are eligible to apply for a 90-day single or multiple-entry e-Visa via the official Vietnam Immigration Portal (`evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn`). Processing takes 3 working days (fee: 25 USD single entry, 50 USD multiple entry).
- **Passport Validity**: Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the arrival date with at least 2 blank pages.

## 2. Transportation & Ride Hailing
- **Grab & Be App**: The most reliable and transparent ride-hailing apps in Hanoi, Da Nang, and Ho Chi Minh City. Cashless credit cards (Visa/Mastercard) can be bound securely.
- **Traditional Metered Taxis**: Stick exclusively to reputable brands: **Mai Linh** (Green taxis - hotline 1055) and **Vinasun** (White with red/green stripes - hotline 028 38 27 27 27). Avoid independent unmarked taxis at airport terminals to prevent meter tampering.
- **Sleeper Buses & Trains**: The Reunification Express train connects Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City. Advance online booking is recommended for soft sleeper 4-berth cabins.

## 3. Currency, SIM Cards & Practical Customs
- **Currency (VND)**: The Vietnamese Dong is a high-denomination currency (e.g. 500,000 VND note). Check note colors carefully: 20,000 VND and 500,000 VND notes share similar blue hues.
- **SIM Cards**: eSims and physical SIMs from Viettel, Vinaphone, and Mobifone can be bought with passport registration at Noi Bai (HAN) and Tan Son Nhat (SGN) airports.
- **Ha Long Bay Environmental Regulations**: Single-use plastics (bottles, bags, straws) are prohibited aboard all overnight cruise vessels and day boats in Ha Long Bay and Lan Ha Bay.

## 4. Emergency Contacts & Safety
- **Police Emergency**: 113.
- **Fire Rescue**: 114.
- **Ambulance Emergency**: 115.
- **Tourist Support Hotline (Hanoi)**: +84 24 3926 1515.
- **Voltage**: 220V, 50Hz. Plug types A, C, and G are commonly supported.

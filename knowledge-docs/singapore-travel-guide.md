# Singapore Travel Guide, Customs & Smart Transit (2025/2026)

## 1. Visa & SG Arrival Card (SGAC)
- **ASEAN Visa Exemption**: Indonesian passport holders and other ASEAN citizens enjoy visa-free entry to Singapore for social and tourism visits for up to 30 days.
- **SG Arrival Card (Mandatory)**: All inbound travelers (including citizens and tourists) must submit the digital SG Arrival Card (SGAC) with electronic health declaration online within 3 days prior to arrival. Submission is free of charge via the official Singapore Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) portal.

## 2. Public Transit, Contactless Payment & Changi Amenities
- **SimplyGo Contactless Payment**: Foreign tourists can tap directly using international Mastercard/Visa contactless credit/debit cards or mobile wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay) at all MRT subway gantries and public buses without purchasing an EZ-Link card.
- **Singapore Tourist Pass (STP)**: Offers unlimited rides on Singapore's basic public bus and MRT train services for 1, 2, or 3 consecutive days (cost: SGD 10 to SGD 20 plus refundable card deposit).
- **Changi Airport Jewel Early Check-in**: Over 30 airlines offer early bag drop and check-in at Jewel Changi Airport up to 12 hours prior to scheduled departure.

## 3. Strict Local Laws, Fines & Prohibited Items
- **Chewing Gum Prohibition**: The import and commercial sale of chewing gum into Singapore is strictly illegal under the Regulation of Imports and Exports Act. Personal importation is prohibited, with penalties starting from SGD 1,000 for littering offenses.
- **Vaping & E-Cigarette Ban**: Possession, use, or importation of vaporizers, e-cigarettes, and e-liquids is strictly prohibited. Fines reach up to SGD 2,000 per offense, and confiscation is immediate at all checkpoints.
- **Littering & Jaywalking**: First-time littering conviction carries a fine up to SGD 2,000 and possible Corrective Work Order (CWO). Jaywalking within 50 meters of a pedestrian crossing incurs an instant SGD 50 fine.

## 4. Tax Refunds (eTRS) & Emergency Contacts
- **eTRS GST Refund**: Tourists spending more than SGD 100 (including GST) at participating retail merchants can claim an 9% GST refund using the electronic Tourist Refund Scheme (eTRS) self-help kiosks located at Changi Airport departure transit lounges before baggage check-in.
- **Police Emergency**: 999.
- **Ambulance & Fire Service**: 995.
- **Non-Emergency Police Hotline**: 1800 255 0000.
- **Power Plugs**: 230V, 50Hz. Plug Type G (British 3-pin standard).

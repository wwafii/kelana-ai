# Japan Travel Insurance, Medication Rules & Shinkansen Luggage Policy (2025/2026)

## 1. Visa Requirements & Visit Japan Web
- **Indonesian Passport Holders**: Indonesian IC e-Passport holders can obtain a pre-registration visa waiver (valid 3 years, max 15 days per visit) free of charge or apply for an eVisa online. Standard non-biometric passport holders must apply for a tourist single/multiple entry visa at the Japan Visa Application Centre (JVAC/VFS Global).
- **Visit Japan Web**: All travelers are advised to fill out customs declarations and immigration fast-track forms online at `vjw-lp.digital.go.jp` to receive two QR codes (Immigration & Customs) for automated kiosk processing at Narita, Haneda, Kansai, and Fukuoka airports.

## 2. Bringing Medication into Japan (Yakkan Shoumei Rules)
- **General Over-the-Counter & Prescription Drugs**: Up to a 1-month supply of standard prescription drugs and up to a 2-month supply of non-prescription medication is permitted without advance permit.
- **Controlled Substances (Psychotropics & Stimulants)**: Medications containing pseudoephedrine (such as Sudafed or certain nasal decongestants), codeine, amphetamines, and medical cannabis/CBD with THC are strictly forbidden under the Stimulants Control Act.
- **Yakkan Shoumei (Import Certificate)**: If carrying more than a 1-month supply of prescription medicine or injectables (e.g., insulin needles), travelers must apply online for an official "Yakkan Shoumei" import approval certificate from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare at least 3 weeks prior to travel.

## 3. Shinkansen Oversized Luggage Rules
- **Dimensions Requirement**: Luggage with total dimensions (Length + Width + Height) between 160 cm and 250 cm requires an advance seat reservation with dedicated oversized baggage area on the Tokaido, Sanyo, and Kyushu Shinkansen lines.
- **Penalty Fee**: Boarding without an oversized luggage reservation incurs a 1,000 JPY penalty fee, and the luggage will be relocated to an area designated by train conductors.
- **Luggage over 250 cm**: Strictly not permitted on Shinkansen trains.

## 4. Cashless Travel Insurance & Emergency Contacts
- **Medical Travel Insurance**: Medical treatment in Japan for foreigners is costly. Japan Tourism Agency strongly urges all travelers to hold comprehensive travel insurance offering direct cashless medical settlement.
- **Japan Visitor Hotline (JNTO)**: 050-3816-2720 (24/7 tourist assistance in English, Chinese, Korean, and Japanese).
- **Police Emergency**: 110.
- **Ambulance & Fire Emergency**: 119.
- **Electricity**: 100V, 50Hz (Eastern Japan/Tokyo) or 60Hz (Western Japan/Osaka). Plug Type A (two flat parallel prongs).

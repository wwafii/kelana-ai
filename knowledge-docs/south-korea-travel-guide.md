# South Korea Travel Guide & Essential Regulations (2025/2026)

## 1. Visa & Entry Requirements
- **K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization)**: Travelers from eligible visa-waiver countries (including eligible business and tourist travelers) must apply for a K-ETA at least 72 hours before boarding their flight. The approved K-ETA is valid for 3 years (or until passport expiration).
- **Indonesian Passport Holders**: Standard Indonesian passport holders require a C-3-9 tourist visa from the Korea Visa Application Center (KVAC) in Jakarta or Bali, unless participating in an authorized group tour to Jeju Island (direct flights) or holding approved transit waivers.
- **Q-CODE (Quarantine Questionnaire)**: Health declaration can be submitted online via Q-CODE prior to departure to expedite Incheon Airport immigration clearance.

## 2. Transportation & Transit Cards
- **T-Money / Climate Card**: The T-Money card is the standard reloadable transportation card for subways, public buses, and designated taxis across Seoul, Busan, and nationwide. Cards can be purchased and reloaded with cash (KRW) at any convenience store (CU, GS25, 7-Eleven) and subway station kiosk.
- **AREX Express Train**: The fastest non-stop transit between Incheon International Airport (Terminal 1 & 2) and Seoul Station (43 minutes). Requires a designated seat ticket.
- **KTX Bullet Train**: For intercity travel (Seoul to Busan in 2.5 hours). Foreign tourists can save by purchasing the Korail Pass in advance.

## 3. Customs, Prohibited Items & Currency
- **Tax Refund Threshold**: Foreign visitors are eligible for instant tax refunds on purchases of 15,000 KRW or more per receipt at certified Tax-Free stores (up to a single transaction limit of 1,000,000 KRW).
- **Prohibited Agricultural Goods**: Fresh meat, sausages, beef jerky, fresh fruit, seeds, and unpasteurized dairy products are strictly prohibited without prior quarantine clearance. Fines for undeclared agricultural products reach up to 10,000,000 KRW.
- **Currency Declaration**: Inbound travelers carrying foreign currency or KRW cash exceeding 10,000 USD equivalent must file an official customs declaration at the border.

## 4. Emergency Contacts & Healthcare
- **Emergency Police**: 112 (English translation available via interpreter link).
- **Fire, Ambulance & Medical Rescue**: 119.
- **Korea Travel Hotline**: 1330 (24/7 multi-language support in Korean, English, Japanese, and Mandarin for travel guidance, translation, and complaint assistance).
- **Voltage & Sockets**: 220V, 60Hz. Plug types C and F (two round pins).
